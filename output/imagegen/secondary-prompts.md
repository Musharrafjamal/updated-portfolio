## Mistribulao

Source: https://mistribulao.in. Navy, cyan/blue, orange-red interface cues. Existing cover inspected; no fictional orange M logo retained.

Use case: product-mockup
 Asset type: premium software portfolio project cover, landscape 3:2, 1536 x 1024 composition.
 Primary request: create one exceptionally refined editorial campaign image for Mistribulao, the real Indian doorstep auto care platform at mistribulao.in. Visually communicate vehicle service booking through a premium smartphone and beautifully photographed car detail.
 Scene: clean architectural studio, seamless cobalt and deep midnight blue backdrop, soft ivory horizontal surface, one understated burnt-orange/coral accent. A cropped front quarter of a silver modern hatchback on the left with crisp real metal reflections and a subtly open hood. No visible car marque or badge.
 Subject: at right, a slim dark graphite smartphone stands on a minimal navy plinth at a very slight perspective, screen facing viewer. On its ivory screen is a polished sparse vehicle-service interface: wordmark "MISTRIBULAO" at top in blue with a small orange gear glyph; a small navy car image; a single clean card "Periodic service"; the only CTA text "Book service" on coral orange. The smartphone should feel like a contemporary digital product, not an infographic. A single small metal workshop tool resting naturally on the surface is enough, no mechanical scatter.
 Art direction: high-end London design studio case-study cover, photorealistic industrial product photography meets outstanding UI presentation. Strong geometry, tactile metal and glass, impeccable controlled shadows, generous empty space. Powerful disciplined composition, large device as hero, car cropped tastefully as secondary physical-world cue. Close attention to device proportions and straight screen geometry.
 Lighting: soft large studio key light, sophisticated reflections, natural contact shadows and restrained depth. No glow effects.
 Palette: source-grounded midnight navy and rich blue, white/ivory interface, warm orange-red service accent.
 Text constraints: only "MISTRIBULAO", "Periodic service", and "Book service"; no external headlines, no taglines, no metrics or explanatory panels.
 Avoid: cartoon mechanic, miniature diorama, floating icons, decorative dotted route, glass callout panels, infographic composition, stickers, stars, fake orange M monogram, crowded dashboard, generic gradients, illegible paragraphs, watermark.
 Output a single finished landscape image.

Source: https://www.freshfieldcrops.com. Authentic source product photo preserved at `output/imagegen/millet-source-product.webp`.

Use case: product-mockup
 Asset type: premium software portfolio project cover, landscape 3:2, 1536 x 1024 composition.
 Primary request: create one exceptionally refined editorial ecommerce case-study cover for the real Millet Fresh Field brand at freshfieldcrops.com, using the authentic reference product as the packaging identity.
 Input image: supporting product reference, not an edit target. It shows the real clear resealable grain pouch: tan printed label, green "Millet Fresh Field" wordmark panel, terracotta pennant, gold grain, Little Millet Grains. Preserve this recognizable authentic packaging style and the exact brand "Millet Fresh Field"; do not invent minimal generic kraft bags.
 Scene: warm ivory studio with a pale travertine table and quiet muted olive background. In the foreground left, one hero real-looking grain pouch using the source identity, plus one smaller secondary pouch partly behind it. A small shallow ivory ceramic bowl holds golden millet grain, with a restrained stalk of millet and a natural linen fold. Minimal artful agricultural details; no full farm landscape, no person.
 At right/back, a floating but physically credible thin desktop browser display in gentle perspective with a precise ivory ecommerce screen. The browser occupies about half the composition. Screen presents the clean green wordmark "Millet Fresh Field", a spacious two-column hero with the short serif heading "From farm to table", and a minimal row of three product thumbnails made from the authentic grain pouch. Only one olive CTA "Shop millets". Sparse layout with generous white space; no dense text. The browser and physical product should be presented as one high-quality composition with layered depth.

 Composition: landscape 3:2. Carefully balanced asymmetry, generous breathing room, tall pouch at left and large horizontal browser at right, surfaces anchored by natural contact shadows. No added cover headline outside the browser.
 Lighting: warm soft window light from upper left, subtle leaf shadow far in backdrop, excellent depth and realistic product highlights.

 Avoid: busy infographic, connecting dotted routes, floating certifications, watercolor collage, cartoon farmer, rustic clutter, glowing glass callouts, generic unbranded kraft packaging, distorted device geometry, dense UI copy, watermark.
